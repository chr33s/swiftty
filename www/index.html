Fig 1

Faster than Ghostty on 16 of 19.

Parser and terminal-state throughput beat Ghostty on 16 of 19 inputs, kitty's benchmark included, up to 3.8× on OSC-heavy output; mixed UTF-8 and unique Unicode cells trail by about 1%. Allocation budgets run in Release.

See the benchmarks
Fig 2

Metal and CoreText. Nothing between.

One serial queue owns the parser and grid; the renderer draws immutable snapshots. Common ASCII, UTF-8, CSI and cell updates make zero steady-state allocations.

Read the architecture
Fig 3

Shells that outlive the window.

A per-user agent owns shells, PTYs, history and layouts. Quitting detaches; tabs and splits grow the same workspace. Reattach from the app or swmux.

Explore SwiftMux
Fig 4

Roam without reconnecting.

RST/1 sends complete current screens over QUIC, not a byte log, so iPhone and iPad sessions survive Wi-Fi and cellular changes. No speculative echo.

~/.config/swiftty/config.jsonc

JSONC with a schema.

Comments, trailing commas and a published $schema for editor completion. Settings writes back to the same file; Reload Configuration applies edits.

SSH keys as identities.

Generate or import Ed25519 keys into the Keychain, device-only by default or opt in to iCloud Keychain. A missing key fails closed and never falls back to a password.

Lower times are better.

Median CPU time of 20 balanced interleaved rounds after two warmups, including startup and file reads in 64 KiB chunks, with Swiftty's history matched to Ghostty's 350 rows. Relative throughput is Ghostty time divided by Swiftty time.
